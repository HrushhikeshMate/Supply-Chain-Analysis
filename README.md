# E-Commerce Delivery Performance & Profitability Analysis

## Business Context
The company operates a global e-commerce platform selling products across diverse categories, including sporting goods, fitness equipment, outdoor gear, footwear, and apparel, across multiple international regions.

## Core Business Problem
Actual shipping times frequently deviate from scheduled delivery windows. This variance leads to:
* **Eroded Customer Trust:** Reduced customer satisfaction and lower repeat purchase rates.
* **Reduced Order Profitability:** Increased costs from customer support, concessions, returns, and expedited re-shipments.
* **Unreliable Commitments:** Inability to provide accurate delivery date promises to buyers at the point of purchase.

## Analytical Objectives
1. **Performance Baseline:** Understand the current state of delivery performance across all dimensions (region, shipping mode, time frame, and customer segment).
2. **Financial Impact Quantification:** Measure the precise impact of delivery delays on overall order profitability.
3. **Bottleneck Identification:** Pinpoint the primary operational and logistical bottlenecks driving late deliveries.
4. **Predictive Risk Modeling:** Build a machine learning model to flag high-risk orders prior to shipment.
5. **Strategic Recommendations:** Deliver actionable, data-driven recommendations to improve on-time delivery rates and optimize order margins.
