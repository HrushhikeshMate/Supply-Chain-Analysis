# Supply-Chain-Analysis
Global E-Commerce Delivery Performance & Logistics Optimization

Overview

The company operates a global e-commerce platform selling products across diverse categories—including sporting goods, fitness equipment, outdoor gear, footwear, and apparel—across multiple international regions.

Core Business Problem

Actual shipping times frequently deviate from scheduled delivery windows. This variance leads to:

Eroded Customer Trust: Unreliable delivery expectations negatively impact customer retention.

Reduced Order Profitability: Delays increase operational costs and support overhead.

Inaccurate Checkout Commitments: Inability to provide reliable delivery estimates to buyers at the point of purchase.

Analytical Objectives

Baseline Delivery Performance: Evaluate the current state of delivery performance across key dimensions, including region, shipping mode, time frame, and customer segment.

Quantify Financial Impact: Measure the precise cost and revenue loss resulting from shipping delays on overall order profitability.

Identify Bottlenecks: Pinpoint the primary operational friction points and root causes driving late deliveries.

Predictive Risk Modeling: Build a machine learning model to flag high-risk orders prior to shipment.

Strategic Recommendations: Deliver data-driven, actionable recommendations to improve on-time delivery metrics and boost profitability.
